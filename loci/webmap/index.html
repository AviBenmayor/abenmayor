A gap address is an eligible address (12+ of 15 categories within 800 m) whose walking distance to this business exceeds its reach tier. A known location is one deduplicated business record that the supply set counts. Single-source records are attested by only one dataset — CHECKPOINT D47 found they inflate apparent supply, so a gap that closes only because of a hollow dot is not yet confirmed. Excluded records (D52) are lone aggregator listings in a category whose licence registry is dense enough to have listed them: the gap model does not count them, and neither does the count above. For a category with no registry anchor loaded nothing is excluded — loading the anchor is the fix, not filtering.
