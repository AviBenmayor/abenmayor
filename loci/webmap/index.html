A gap address is any residential address whose walking distance to this business exceeds its reach tier — every address counts, the 12-of-15 walkability gate having been retired on 2026-09-13 (CHECKPOINT D75). Walking distances are measured out to 2,400 m and no further; an address flagged beyond 2,400 m has nothing of this kind inside that radius and its score is a floor, not a measurement. A known location is one deduplicated business record that the supply set counts. Single-source records are attested by only one dataset — CHECKPOINT D47 found they inflate apparent supply, so a gap that closes only because of a hollow dot is not yet confirmed. Excluded records (D52) are lone aggregator listings in a category whose licence registry is dense enough to have listed them: the gap model does not count them, and neither does the count above. For a category with no registry anchor loaded nothing is excluded — loading the anchor is the fix, not filtering.
