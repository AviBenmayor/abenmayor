## 4. What this licenses for the AC-1 decision

NYC leads the wave rather than following it. Per-visit communal openings 2021–2026 in the comparison cities: Chicago, LA and DC 0; Miami 0–1; Austin 1–2; Philadelphia 1 (2026-09-25, a Bathhouse export). NYC's 12 in the same span is the national signal; the rest is capital and announcements. There is no external base rate to borrow.

A Gowanus entrant competes with three rows in §2: one operating L-class chain site 0.8 km away, open four months, entry $29; a suspended local incumbent 230 m away whose bathing offer went dark; and a 2028 gym on the canal with rooftop bathing priced into a membership. The pipeline adds two to four Brooklyn sites before 2027-Q2 (Reunion and SaunaLounge on filings; SUND and a possible Bathhouse NYC site on press), none inside 2 km.

What the number licenses: scoring a dated prediction. At 1.7 per year, twelve months yields 1.7 expected citywide openings and an 18% chance of zero; a registered count outside 0.9–2.9, or pipeline rows named by address, is a call that can be wrong. A catchment rate (~0.01 per km²-year) cannot be, which is R1's reason. What it does not license: any statement that Gowanus is under- or over-supplied; that is the trade-area sheet (D116, AC-1 Step 2) and its 10/15/20/30-minute sweep. The 3.4 acceleration is momentum, not a floor: the 2019+ cohort has no closures because it is young.

## 5. Coverage and censoring

- The first-seen ledger alone dates 6 of 13 in-window openings: 0.78 per year (0.29–1.70), a floor.
- Raw feeds carry 12 of 14 venues, but FSQ "Bath House"/"Sauna" and Overture `sauna`/`onsen` were unmapped at the read and never reached `staging.poi`; Lore and Connexion are absent outright. GTM-198 maps them going forward.
- A first-seen within ±3 months of the press date exists for 9 of 14. FSQ `date_created` is a listing date and leads openings (World Spa 3.7 years, QC NY 4 months); DOHMH `gov_filing` trails 0–5 months.
- The 2016–2018 zero is partly a search artefact: outer-borough Korean and Chinese water spas rarely get English press. Woori Healing Sauna (NYS licence 2026-01) may be an uncounted 2026 opening; seven Queens saunas in `candidates.csv` are unresolved.
- Unverified: Schwet's public opening; open status of Neck Road, Mermaid, Brooklyn Banya, Fountain of Youth; The SPA Club's date and relation to K-Town Sauna; Brooklyn Bathhouse and The Altar to the month; cityWell's pause date; Sandoony and Premier 57 closure years (web and FSQ disagree by 2–5 years); bathing-primary status of the three R2 day spas; SaunaLounge's format; Reunion's "winter 2026" versus the D80 2027-Q2.
