# RQ-002 — Data audit

Read-only audit of `src/loci/registry.yaml` and `data/loci.duckdb` against the question's
five pillars (TOWERS / BUSINESSES / RESIDENTIAL RENT & DEMAND / COMMERCIAL RENT / MACRO
& CONTROL) for ZIP 11222 (Greenpoint), plus a GEOGRAPHY pillar the others all depend on.
Warehouse opened `read_only=True` at `data/loci.duckdb` (102 tables) — connection
succeeded, no lock encountered, nothing written. Probed 2026-09-22. Every `missing`/
`partial` row's Gap column follows the framework's rule for this stage: it names a
one-line ticket title, not a fabricated GTM id — `src/loci/tickets.py` was grepped for
each pillar's keywords (`grep -n -iE "greenpoint|11222|zori|zhvi|lodes|rebny|decennial|
commercial_listing|rezoning|macro"`) and only `GTM-137` (DOF storefront registry) and
`GTM-212` (the on-hold commercial-listings/public-signals commit) resolve to real,
already-defined tickets; the several ZORI/LODES/ACS/macro gaps below are the SAME
underlying warehouse gaps RQ-001's `DATA-AUDIT.md` already found and proposed tickets for
(`RQ001-zori-zhvi-ingest`, `RQ001-lodes-ingest`, `RQ001-acs-panel-backfill`,
`RQ001-macro-ingest`) — none of those four have been pushed to `tickets.py` yet either
(grepped, zero hits), so this file flags them as "TICKET NEEDED" too and notes the
RQ-001 cross-reference so step 8 does not create a duplicate ingest ticket for the same
gap under two different RQ names.

## v0 feasibility

**The TOWERS pillar is fully answerable today.** `analysis.dev_pipeline` (NYC DCP Housing
Database, already in the warehouse, 48,762 citywide / 1,033 Greenpoint rows) gives a
real BBL-level timeline of every filed/permitted/completed residential job in 11222 from
2000 onward — 321 New Building filings, +14,372 net units, with filed/permitted/complete
dates and CO type per tower. This alone answers "when did the towers go up" and "tower-by-
tower first occupancy" (via `date_complete`, populated for 237 of 321 rows) without any new
ingest. Cross-checking the earliest rows (pre-2010, before the Housing Database's nominal
start) against classic DOB BIS filings, and adding a small number of hand-verified press
citations for the flagship parcels, would round this out but is not blocking.

**The BUSINESSES pillar is partially answerable today, fully answerable after one join
ticket.** `census_zbp`/`census_cbp` already give a real 1998–2023 ZIP×NAICS establishment
panel natively keyed to `zipcode='11222'` — the aggregate "did the business count increase"
question is answerable right now with zero new ingest. The harder pioneer-vs-follower
ordering question needs `analysis.poi_first_seen` (1978–2026, 219,458 rows, real per-POI
open dates) filtered down to 11222 and to retail/food/service categories — but that table
has NO `zip` column, and NEITHER DOES ANY OTHER POI-grain table in the warehouse
(`poi_presence`, `licence_interval`, `analysis.address` all key on borough/NTA/lon-lat, not
ZIP), and there is no ZCTA boundary polygon table loaded to do the spatial join. This
GEOGRAPHY gap is the single blocking dependency for turning any address-level source into
an 11222-scoped answer, and it blocks four separate rows in the table above at once.

**RESIDENTIAL RENT is the weakest pillar and the only one with NOTHING pre-2010.** ZORI/
ZHVI (would reach 2000) is registered but has zero rows; ACS is a single 2023 cross-section
with no rent field loaded at all (median gross rent isn't even in the schema, let alone as
a series); and there is no decennial Census source registered anywhere in the project,
meaning nothing in Loci today reaches the question's own 1994–2010 pre-trend window for any
demand variable. **This is the hardest blocker for the control-ZIP pre-trend match specifically**
— it cannot be built from data already in the warehouse.

**COMMERCIAL RENT has no usable free source for 11222 today, and may not get one.** DOF
storefront vacancy (present, GTM-137) is a vacancy proxy with no rent figure and only a
7-year window. REBNY's Brooklyn report is the one plausible free corridor-rent source and
it is UNVERIFIED whether it has ever named a Greenpoint corridor at all — that needs
checking before investing in the ingest. The one commercial-listings collector that could
plausibly carry asking rents (`commercial_listings.py`) exists on disk but is uncommitted,
unregistered, and explicitly on hold (GTM-212) pending a peer confirmation unrelated to
this RQ. DOF assessment/RPAD rolls (a value proxy) are not registered at all. **v0 should
state plainly that commercial rent for 11222 is very likely unanswerable from free public
data**, and scope the notebook's commercial-rent claim accordingly (vacancy trend as the
best available proxy, explicitly flagged as not rent) rather than block on an ingest that
may not resolve the question anyway.

**MACRO/control-ZIP infrastructure is half-built, not absent** — `src/loci/sources/macro.py`
already has 11 verified FRED series and a COVID-window constant from RQ-001's build, just
not registered or warehouse-persisted; reusing it is cheap and should happen before anyone
re-derives the same FRED ids for RQ-002.

**Net verdict:** a v0 answer can ship TODAY on towers (full timeline) and on the aggregate
retail business-count trend (ZBP/CBP, 1998–2023). Pioneer/follower ordering needs one
GEOGRAPHY (ZCTA join) ticket before it can run. Residential rent trend needs ZORI ingested
at minimum to say anything about rent since 2015; the pre-2010 half of the question (both
residential rent and the control-ZIP pre-trend match) is blocked on the decennial-Census gap
and should be marked BLOCKED in ANSWER.md rather than improvised from a proxy. Commercial
rent should be answered with the honest caveat that no qualifying free source currently
reaches 11222, using DOF vacancy as the closest available (non-rent) signal.
