## 6. Corroboration questionnaire — ask in this order

1. **What business was at 379 Broome, and what years?** Café/coffee, full restaurant, or something
   else entirely? *(tests prediction (a) — the whole category call)*
2. **Trailing-twelve-month gross revenue** at its best full year (a range is fine). Net of sales
   tax? Third-party delivery gross or net of commission? *(tests (b) — the cap)*
3. **Monthly base rent and square feet leased.** PLUTO says 2,013 sq ft of retail split over two
   stores — was your demise the whole thing or half? *(tests (b) and the supportable-rent verdict)*
4. **Weekday vs weekend split of revenue**, and the busiest hour of the week. *(tests (c))*
5. **Who did you actually compete with?** Name three. Were any of the names in (d) not real
   competitors, and are any of them shut today? *(tests (d))*
6. **On this block, when a storefront empties, how fast does it re-let?** Weeks, months, years?
   *(tests (e) — churn vs vacancy)*
7. **Where did customers come from** — residents, office workers, tourists, transit riders? Roughly
   what split? *(tests whether a 6,077-home catchment or a 19,426-job catchment was the business)*
8. **Was a bodega/convenience store ever viable on this block**, and if not, why not? *(tests the
   lead category directly — a gap that a peer lacks for a reason is not an opportunity)*
9. **Was the space ever under LOI to a national or chain operator** while you were there? *(pipeline
   check — outside the data entirely)*
10. **What killed it, or what would have?** Rent, labour, footfall, or the concept.

### What would change our mind, per prediction

- **(a)** A non-food business at the address, or a food business in a category Loci scores thin →
  the category read is wrong at the address grain, not merely imprecise.
- **(b)** Revenue above the p50 cap → the capacity ceiling is too tight for Manhattan corridors and
  the whole `capacity_psf_per_year` band needs a NYC-specific re-cut.
- **(c)** Weekdays materially stronger → transit entries at 400 m are the wrong daypart instrument
  for a mixed job/tourist district and the AM/PM share signal (D76) mis-classifies SoHo.
- **(d)** ≥3 missing real competitors → a coverage hole at a site whose categories grade **A**
  (restaurant 1.0%, cafe_bakery 1.5%) — which would be worse news than a hole in a C category.
- **(e)** Long vacancies rather than fast churn → the D88 "ranks retail streets" reading does not
  carry to the block grain, and the cost-of-search claim weakens.

## 7. Caveats — read before treating anything above as a forecast

- **Every category on this card is grade D overall.** Nothing here says *act*. Arriving-homes is D
  everywhere (permit renewal is not a shovel); economics is D in 13 of 15 (n_comps 1–4, no
  cash-flow data, `rent_source: listed`). Laundry alone reaches C on economics.
- **Revenue is absent, not merely stale.** `revenue_p50` is NULL in all 45,862 MN rows of
  `analysis.address_category`. The anticipated hash-drift caveat still stands underneath it —
  `revenue_calibration.yaml` is stamped `supply_hash: 767b28674e30`, the live screen is
  `9a11a2f5b9e7` — but it is moot while nothing has been applied. The §5 caps come from PLUTO and
  the published psf bands, not from the fitted model; the comps figures are listings.
- **Age-fit NULL** (stale-curve refusal): no demographic-fit re-ranking behind the lead category,
  and `gap_score_fit` is unavailable, so the score is unadjusted distance-and-supply.
- **Coverage grades for this site's categories** (`docs/coverage-validation-2026-09.md`, G9 Wilson-
  upper ladder, D90): restaurant **A** (1.0%, upper 3.6%), cafe_bakery **A** (1.5%, 4.3%),
  convenience **A** (0.0%, 1.5%), laundry **A** (1.5%, 4.2%), **grocery B**. Per D90, hair_barber,
  tailor_repair and clinic are **demoted from headline claims** (C) — three of the fifteen rows in
  §3 should not carry weight. City-wide P3 survives at 7.6% [6.5–8.8], no income gradient.
- **DCWP `out_of_business` is under review for laundry.** The laundry anchor comes from DCWP
  Inspections (2,277 MN+BK establishments staged, 1,561 active, 1,289 flagged out_of_business); the
  feed starts 2023-07, so a never-inspected laundry is indistinguishable from a gap. Laundry's
  1.81× here is the weakest supply number in the table.
- **`unknown` is the modal POI status** in the 400 m disc (996 of 1,904); seven whole categories have no open-evidence source here. And the 400 m disc and the 400 m network reach disagree by 2.6× on restaurants (665 vs 252) — cite the network number in anything that leaves this file.
- **D1 stands.** The causal "gaps predict growth" thesis was rejected (β = +0.069, p = 4.7e-16,
  wrong sign; parallel trends broken). Nothing in this record is an appreciation claim. Loci ranks
  present-day opportunity and cost of search, and at this address it ranks it at zero.
- **Pipeline diligence is outside the data.** Whether 379 Broome is under LOI today is a phone call, not a query.
