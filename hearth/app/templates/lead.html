What the model itself weighted (conditional contributions)

The score is a regularised logistic regression, so its coefficients are conditional: each one is the effect of that field after the others are accounted for. That is why a revenue band can carry a negative coefficient — the campaign and ICP dummies have already taken the credit. Useful for debugging the model, misleading as a reason to give a rep, which is why the panel above uses observed segment rates instead.

What this does not know

Twelve intake columns exist at the moment a lead lands. The score reads five of them — revenue band, ICP, campaign, time zone and the legacy score — because dropping channel, utm_medium and state improved out-of-time AUC, and those are the fields that drift hardest between windows. enrichment_status is dead in both windows and zip3 is too sparse to fit. The score cannot see anything a human learned on a call, because none of the {{ '{:,}'.format(cap.leads) }} scored leads have call data. It does not know whether this contractor is real, whether the number connects, or whether they already bought. Treat it as where to spend the next hour, not as a verdict — and if you work a C or a D and it converts, that is a data point I want, not a violation.
